What to look for

  • Unexpected application or registration fees
  • Unverified sender addresses or domains
  • Pressure to respond immediately
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs or sensitive details
  • Claims that cannot be confirmed independently

This tool offers basic text-based guidance, not a definitive fraud detection service.
